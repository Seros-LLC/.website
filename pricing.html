Pricing · planned

Priced per seat. Predictable per month.

Annual is two months free — pay for ten, get twelve. Viewers are free and unlimited: only people who confirm, own, or receive rollups need a seat.

What counts as an AI action

One AI action is one model-backed piece of work that produces something you see: a detection pass that finds a task, a task draft or redraft, an owner suggestion, or a generated summary. Actions are pooled across your workspace, so a ten-seat Team account shares 3,500 a month.

Not counted: confirming, editing, or rejecting a suggestion; syncing status with your tracker; reminders and nudges; and retries caused by our own errors or a provider outage. You do not pay for our mistakes.
