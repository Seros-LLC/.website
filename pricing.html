Early access

Start with a real workflow, not a pricing promise.

Seros is in private early access. We are working with teams that want to turn Slack commitments into reviewed Linear issues without giving automation permission to write on its own.

What early access includes today

Choose the Slack channels

An owner or admin connects Slack, then selects the exact channels Seros may read. Unticked channels stay out of scope.

Review every draft

Seros identifies possible commitments and puts them in a queue. A person can edit, confirm, or reject each one.

Create a Linear issue only after confirmation

No draft becomes tracker work until a permitted person confirms it. The audit log records the outcome without storing message content.

How early access works

  1. Create a workspace for your team.
  2. Connect Slack and select the exact channels Seros may read.
  3. Review drafts before anything reaches Linear.

Early access is available through account creation. There is no checkout or self-service plan selection yet.

What comes later

We may publish plans, prices, broader integrations, and enterprise controls after we have validated the workflow. They are not available or committed on this page today.
