What we build.

Seros is a solution development company. We take a business problem that is currently handled by spreadsheets, copy-paste, or nobody, and we turn it into software that is specified in writing, built to a fixed scope, and maintained after it ships.

Four ways to work with us

Discovery sprint

One week, fixed fee. We interview the people doing the work, map the current process, and deliver a written specification: what gets built, what it touches, what it costs, and what we deliberately leave out. You own the document whether or not we build it.

Custom build

Internal tools, dashboards, portals, back-office workflows. Fixed scope from the discovery document, weekly working demos, deployed to infrastructure you own and control.

Systems integration

Connect the tools you already pay for so a fact is entered once. Chat, trackers, CRMs, accounting systems, and internal databases, joined by code we write and document.

Care plan

A monthly retainer after delivery: dependency and security updates, monitoring, a named contact for breakage, and a fixed allowance of hours for small changes.

AI features, with the safety rails on

We build AI into products the way we build it for ourselves: a model may propose, a person decides, and every consequential write is recorded. We will tell you plainly when a problem does not need a model at all — that answer is free, and it is often the right one.

How an engagement runs

  1. Scoping call. Thirty minutes. You describe the problem, we say whether we are the right people to solve it.
  2. Discovery sprint. A paid week that ends in a written specification and a price for the build.
  3. Build. Fixed scope, weekly demo, work visible in your repository.
  4. Handover. Documentation, credentials, and a walkthrough with your team. The code is yours.
  5. Care plan. Optional. Monthly, cancellable.

What we do not do

Staff augmentation by the body. Open-ended time-and-materials projects with no written scope. Marketing sites. Anything where nobody on your side owns the outcome.
