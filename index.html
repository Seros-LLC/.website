One clear route from conversation to accountable work.

Seros makes the hand-off visible rather than silently automating it. The source stays connected to the proposed task all the way through review.

  1. 01

    Set the boundary

    An owner or admin connects Slack, then chooses the exact channels Seros may read.

    Scope set
  2. 02

    Find the commitment

    Seros compares what was promised in scope with what actually made it into Linear.

    Signal found
  3. 03

    Review the draft

    A person can inspect and edit the proposed outcome, owner, and due date before deciding.

    Human gate
  4. 04

    Confirm the write

    Only a confirmed draft becomes a Linear issue. The original conversation remains attached.
