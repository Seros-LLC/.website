The problem

Your tracker cannot track work that never gets entered.

A commitment in Slack is not an issue. Until someone notices it, writes it up, chooses an owner and links the context, your plan is incomplete. Seros makes that gap visible first, then gives a delivery manager a faster way to close it.

How it works

Measure. Draft. Confirm. Track.

A human approves every assignment. Seros drafts and proposes; your team decides. We are not building an autopilot for your business, and we say so in our terms.

  1. 1

    Capture

    Choose Slack channels. Seros finds commitments and compares them with what reached Linear.

  2. 2

    Draft

    It drafts the Linear issue: outcome, context, acceptance criteria and the source link.

  3. 3

    Assign

    It proposes an owner and due date only when the conversation supports them. A human confirms — always.

  4. 4

    Track

    The confirmed issue is created in Linear. The original conversation remains attached.

Who it is for

Built first for Slack and Linear teams with a hand-off problem.

  • Product and delivery teams whose work begins in Slack but is planned in Linear.
  • Technical agencies and dev shops managing several client conversations.
  • Operations leaders who suspect the board shows less work than the team is actually doing.
  • Founders and delivery leads who are still the bottleneck for every hand-off.
