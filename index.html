Delegation is a discipline. We made it a system.

The problem

Work does not stall on effort. It stalls on hand-off.

Every small team loses hours in the same places: a request buried in a thread, a task nobody owns, a status nobody can find, the same instructions typed again for the third time. Project tools assume the work is already defined. The expensive part is defining it.

01

Requests get lost

Asks arrive in chat, email, calls, and tickets. Most never become a task at all.

02

Ownership is vague

“Someone should…” is not an assignment. Work without an owner does not move.

03

Context is retyped

The person doing the work rebuilds the background every single time.

How it works

Capture. Draft. Assign. Track.

A human approves every assignment. Seros drafts and proposes; your team decides. We are not building an autopilot for your business, and we say so in our terms.

  1. 1

    Capture

    Connect the places work is asked for. Seros reads the request, not just the words.

  2. 2

    Draft

    It writes the task: outcome, context, acceptance criteria, effort, and a first pass at priority.

  3. 3

    Assign

    It proposes an owner based on skills, load, and history. A human confirms — always.

  4. 4

    Track

    Nudges, status roll-ups, and a weekly view of what actually moved.

Who it is for

Built for teams of 10 to 200 who run on hand-offs.

  • Agencies and studios juggling client requests across a dozen channels.
  • Managed service and IT teams where intake quality decides the week.
  • Operations, finance, and back-office teams drowning in recurring asks.
  • Founders who are still the bottleneck for every decision about who does what.
